01Summary

Applyfolio is a website I made to keep the different parts of a job search in one place. You can save jobs from any source, track applications, record deadlines and next steps, and keep notes and networking contacts connected to the positions they relate to.

It is designed to be easy to look at and understand. The dashboard shows what you have saved, applied to, or are interviewing for, while bringing upcoming tasks and follow-ups into view. You can also compare offers using salary and living-cost estimates. The goal is to see where everything stands without constantly switching between job sites, emails, spreadsheets, and documents.

02Problem

A job search rarely happens in one place.

Applications are submitted through different websites, and each company has its own deadlines and interview process. At the same time, conversations with employees and recruiters happen through email, LinkedIn, and other channels. Those connections matter, but they can become difficult to keep track of once several opportunities are moving at the same time.

I wanted a straightforward way to see the full picture: which jobs need attention, what comes next, and who I have already spoken with. Rather than another place to find jobs, Applyfolio focuses on keeping track of the search once it has started.

03Target users

Applyfolio is for anyone actively applying to multiple jobs and managing the deadlines, tasks, and contacts that come with them. I initially had students in mind, particularly those recruiting across several industries where applications open at different times and follow different processes. The same need exists for people changing jobs or exploring opportunities at any career stage.

04Solution

The site is organized around a simple process: add a job, keep its details and connections together, and use the dashboard to see what needs to happen next. The examples below show the main screens.

01Add a job

Save the basic information for a position, including the deadline, application status, and next action. Jobs can be added manually or by pasting a posting link, which Applyfolio attempts to read when the website allows it.

05Distribution

To start sharing the project, I put up QR codes around campus linking directly to Applyfolio and sent it to student clubs whose members are actively recruiting. I also shared it beyond campus with students at other universities, including Tufts, MIT, and UCLA.

The next step is broader outreach beyond college campuses so I can see how useful the product is for people at other career stages who are also managing multiple applications, deadlines, and professional connections at once.
