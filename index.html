Compare opportunities, not just salary

Translate compensation into what the job means in real life. The product shows tradeoffs instead of telling the user which offer is “better.”

Offer A

Product Analyst · New York, NY

Base salary$115,000
Sign-on$10,000
Est. monthly take-home$6,180
Rent assumption$2,400
After fixed costs$2,130/mo
First-year cash comp$125,000

Offer B

Strategy Associate · San Francisco, CA

Base salary$130,000
Sign-on$5,000
Est. monthly take-home$6,650
Rent assumption$3,100
After fixed costs$2,050/mo
First-year cash comp$135,000
Example interpretation: Offer B pays more on paper, but under the assumptions entered here, it leaves slightly less each month after fixed costs. The job seeker still makes the decision.
